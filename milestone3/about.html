About Me

My name is Talfor, an indie dev currently in College, aiming for a Bachelor's in Computer Science. This website is merely a personal project I intend to place my creations on for other people to enjoy.

Is it safe?
Yes. I'm aware of how terrible the internet can be. People will put viruses, malware, scareware, or scams everywhere. My intention is to not scam people. All my projects are personally developed and are safe to use. Want proof? They're all open source! Each program, mod, tool, or project is completely open, and if anyone ever finds a project is malware, I will personally sue myself and give you the money.
Which won't happen, because it's not malware.
But if I'm being honest, that really isn't enough, is it?
So, I encourage you, know what you're downloading before you do. Do your research, use the project offline at first on a virtual machine, make 100% sure you can trust what you're using.

Is it quality?
As much as we can. All my projects are personally hand developed without an AI or plagerism, but I'm not a professional expert, nor is Krameri Studios a big team. Naturally, this means that there are going to be bugs and massive room for improvement. However, the intent is that any 1.0 releases are relatively bugfree and as high quality, clean and professional as I can make them.

Can I beta test?
Yes. Pre-1.0 Releases exist. Go check them out if you're interested!
