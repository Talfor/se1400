The Brimstone Witch, Supreme Calamitas

Minecraft/Terraria Calamity Boss Mod
A high end Minecraft boss fight mod that adds the final boss of Terraria Calamity: Supreme Calamitas.
This mod is designed to perfectly match the original fight. You can use the Terraria Calamity Wiki to see everything she does.
Includes the Brimstone Crags, unique ores and weapons, and drops from the boss. You can also choose difficulty upon summoning her.

Disclaimer: Understand I do not support the controversial creator we have been forced to face right now. This mod is not made for him, but in respect for the other creators who have faced difficulty because of him.

2nd Disclaimer: I am not an artist. The models, though my work, are not going to be crazy exceptional. Also, a lot of assets are taken from Calamity. I do intend to remake them as my own work eventually. Credit to the Calamity team for said assets & concepts.

3rd Disclaimer: This mod is in beta. Optimization, balancing, and other tweaks are on the way.
